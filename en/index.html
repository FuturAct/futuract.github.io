Perspective

Bringing the future into the present. From where an institution stands, many futures open. We turn knowledge into agency: the capacity to choose one of them and give it body.

Strategies rarely fail in the diagnosis. They fail in the passage to action.

Agency

Institutions rarely lack information. What they often lack is agency: the capacity to turn what they know into decisions and coordinated action. Building that capacity, inside the institution, is the purpose of our work.

Who we are

Different perspectives. Shared purpose.

FuturAct is a consultancy founded in Brazil, working internationally.

We combine extensive experience in innovation policy, productive development, strategic design, institutional transformation and applied research.

What sets us apart: we bring an economist’s understanding of innovation systems and clusters together with a designer’s practice of making programmes work on the ground, from strategy to the identity, communication and digital interfaces that make people use them.

We work directly with institutions and in consortia with partner organisations, on projects of different scales and durations.

How we work

Understand complexity. Design possibilities. Enable action.

Four steps, from the first conversation to capabilities that stay in the institution. Each one ends with something concrete in your hands.

Engage and diagnose

Map actors, capabilities and bottlenecks with the people involved.

You get a shared diagnosis and clear priorities.

Co-design

Translate strategy into programme architecture, services and governance, designed with those who will run them.

You get programme design, service blueprints and a governance model.

Pilot and implement

Test before scaling and work alongside teams, giving the programme an identity, communication and digital channels people actually use.

You get tested pilots, a programme identity and launch, and a roadmap by horizon.

Evaluate and learn

Realist evaluation and contribution analysis: what works, for whom and why.

You get evidence for decisions and capabilities that stay in the institution.

For cluster organisations and innovation agencies, this often means

  • Turning a cluster roadmap into projects and services members actually use.
  • Designing support services for members and startups.
  • Giving clusters and programmes an identity, communication and digital channels members engage with.
  • Evaluating cluster and innovation programmes.
  • Building the capabilities of cluster managers and programme teams.

The founders

Maria Augusta Pimentel Miglino

Innovation Policy · Productive Development · Programme Design & Evaluation

Maria Augusta is a senior expert in innovation and productive development, with nearly 30 years at the intersection of innovation systems, territorial development and science, technology and innovation policy. Her work spans the full cycle of public programmes: research and diagnosis, evaluation, programme design, implementation and governance.

Over 16 years at Sebrae-SP she worked on cluster development and competitiveness, designed and oversaw complex innovation programmes and managed the state deep-tech programmes Start IA, Start Bio and Start Deeptech. She presented research on clusters at the 15th TCI Global Conference (2012).

More

  • Coordinator of the Startups do Futuro award (2022 to 2023): 531 science-based startups mapped and 103 recognised as the most promising.
  • State coordinator of the Incubadora-SP programme at Sebrae-SP.

  • Papers on local innovation systems and clusters at EUNIP conferences (2001 to 2003), the 15th TCI Global Conference (2012) and the Triple Helix Conference (2024).

Leandro Pasini

Strategic & Service Design · Public Innovation · Evaluation

Leandro is a strategic and service designer working on public innovation and institutional capacity. After a career in communication and design, since 2014 he has turned complex problems into programmes, services and governance arrangements, combining systems thinking, qualitative research and collaborative facilitation with public institutions, foundations and territorial networks.

He conceived and facilitated a local innovation ecosystems initiative in ten territories of São Paulo state, co-developed the Design Doing methodology and, in 2026, evaluated the programmes of São Paulo City’s public innovation lab for UNESCO.

More

Selected experience

  • Sebrae-SP, innovation projects specialist (2021 to 2026): local innovation ecosystems in ten territories, the São Paulo agricultural innovation corridor with Embrapa, APTA and universities, and the Barretos healthtech ecosystem.
  • Instituto Tellus, service designer (2014 to 2021): secondary-education public policy with Fundação Itaú in Mato Grosso do Sul, Paraíba and Amapá, including the training of 60 state network managers; ReDUS platform with GIZ; InovaJuntos public policy lab; the StartOut Brasil internationalisation programme with MDIC, ANPEI and the British Embassy.
  • Earlier career in digital creative direction and advertising (Ogilvy, Leo Burnett, JWT, F/Nazca), including one of the first Brazilian internet banking projects, in 1999.
  • Ongoing personal research on AI-enabled knowledge architectures for evidence organisation and decision support.
